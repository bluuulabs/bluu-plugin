# Month in review

Use the Bluu tools to review the month the user names, or last month if they name none:

1. What came in and what went out.
2. The categories that moved the most.
3. Budgets they went over.
4. Progress on their goals.

Report totals per currency and never add different currencies together. Describe what happened; do not tell them what to do with their money. Keep it short, and end by offering the Bluu link from the results.
