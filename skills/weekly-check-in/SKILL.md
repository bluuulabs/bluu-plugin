# Weekly money check-in

Use the Bluu tools to give the user their week, for one space if they name it, otherwise for all of them:

1. What they spent this week.
2. What they have to pay in the next seven days.
3. Any budget they are over.
4. How their goals are pacing.
5. At most one thing that stands out against their own usual, said as what you see, never as what they should do.

Report totals per currency and never add different currencies together. Keep it short, and end by offering the Bluu link from the results so they can see the details there.
